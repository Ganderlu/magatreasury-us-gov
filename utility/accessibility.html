U.S. DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

Accessibility Statement

The U.S. Department of the Treasury is committed to making its electronic and information technologies accessible to individuals with disabilities in accordance with Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (29 U.S.C. 794d).

Our Commitment

Section 508 is a federal law that requires agencies to provide individuals with disabilities equal access to electronic information and data comparable to those who do not have disabilities, unless an undue burden would be imposed on the agency.

Standards We Follow

We strive to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. Our ongoing accessibility efforts include:

  • Providing text alternatives (alt text) for non-text content.
  • Ensuring full keyboard navigability across the entire site.
  • Maintaining high color contrast ratios for all text and UI elements.
  • Using descriptive headings and labels to support screen reader users.
  • Regularly testing our site with assistive technologies.

Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you have difficulty accessing any information or using any feature on this website, please let us know. Your feedback helps us improve the experience for everyone.

Contact the Accessibility Team:

Email: accessibility@magatreasury.com

Response time: We typically respond to accessibility inquiries within 2 business days.
